CDI-linked investment calculator

Estimate returns on a CDI-linked investment by entering the amount, term and CDI percentage.

How to use

Enter the requested values and click calculate. The result appears in your browser.

The simulation depends on the entered CDI rate, contracted percentage, term, contributions and applicable taxes. Check current official data before making a decision.

How to interpret the result

The result depends on the values entered and the method shown on this page. Check units, time period and assumptions before using the number for a decision.
