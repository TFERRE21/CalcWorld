Dividend yield calculator

Calculate dividend yield from price and dividends.

How to use

Enter the requested values and click calculate. The result appears in your browser.

How to interpret the result

The result depends on the values entered and the method shown on this page. Check units, time period and assumptions before using the number for a decision.

About this calculator

This page shows the inputs, the applicable formula or method, and a simple interpretation of the result.

Formula / method

Dividend yield = annual dividends ÷ price × 100
