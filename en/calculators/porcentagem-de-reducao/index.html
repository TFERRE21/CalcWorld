Percentage reduction

Calculate percentage reduction.

How to use

Enter the requested values and click calculate. The result appears in your browser.

Check units and operation order. If the result seems unexpected, repeat the calculation with smaller values to verify each step.

Worked example

A 200 item with a 15% discount saves 30 and costs 170 after the discount (200 × (1 − 0.15)).

About this calculator

This page shows the inputs, the applicable formula or method, and a simple interpretation of the result.

Formula / method

Final price = price × (1 − discount ÷ 100)

Important

This tool is informational. Verify applicable rules for legal, financial, tax or health calculations.

Understand discounts

The percentage is applied to the original price to show savings and final price. Successive discounts should be calculated sequentially.
